## 1. Purpose

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") governs the
exchange of confidential information between Acme Corporation ("Acme")
and Globex Industries ("Globex") for the purpose of evaluating a
potential business transaction.

## 2. Term

The term of this Agreement is two (2) years from the Effective Date.

## 3. Confidentiality Obligations

Each party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in strict
confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without the
disclosing party's prior written consent.

## 4. Notices

All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in
writing and delivered to the addresses on the cover page.

## 5. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its
conflict-of-laws principles.
